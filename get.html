Open in your browser to install

Tap the ⋮ or … menu at the top and choose Open in browser.

Install on Android

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right of Chrome
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app
  3. Tap Install. Musaafir GPS appears on your home screen

Install on iPhone

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. Tap Add. Musaafir GPS appears on your home screen
